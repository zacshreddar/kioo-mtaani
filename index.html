Issue 01: The culture issue

This magazine belongs to the mtaa.

KIOO MTAANI points the camera at the culture that makes our neighbourhoods what they are: the Sheng poet, the matatu artist, the stars on the world stage and the places where Nairobi spends its nights.

Every portrait is shot with the dignity of a magazine cover. Every story is told in the subject’s own voice, in Sheng, Swahili or English.
